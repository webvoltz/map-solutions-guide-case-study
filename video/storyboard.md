# Storyboard

1920 × 1080, 30 fps, 63 s. A cartographic look, unlike Webvoltz's other videos: ink-navy chart paper, cream serif type (Fraunces), mono coordinate labels (IBM Plex Mono), drifting contour lines generated in code, a graticule, and a globe drawn from Natural Earth coastlines. Data colours are teal, gold and coral. Real product footage is shown only inside a "map sheet" frame with tick marks, cropped to the map, with the Mapbox and OpenStreetMap credit.

| Time | Scene | On screen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | **Every decision has a where** | Contour lines drift over a graticule. "Every decision has a where." Crosshair markers land one by one with coordinates: insure, build, deliver, the risk is. |
| 0:07 | **Rows, not patterns** | A CSV of addresses on chart paper. Grey dots scatter across the map, then gather into three coloured clusters: "…until it's on a map." |
| 0:12 | **What we build** | A coastline globe draws itself and turns, with pulsing points around the world. "Map products that turn location into decisions." |
| 0:17 | **Draw zones** | Cream paper. Real footage of a zone being drawn, then a 100 m grid cell, with notes for free-form polygons, grid cells (100 m to 1 km) and area measured instantly. |
| 0:25 | **Score and colour** | A generated grid of cells colours itself in a sweep (teal, gold, coral) while a rating counts up to 5/6; the legend appears, then real footage of coloured zones. |
| 0:31 | **Explore** | Coordinates type into a search box; a Street/Satellite toggle flips; real footage flies to a zone, then pulls back to the globe. |
| 0:37 | **Location lookup** | A pin drops on the globe with a coordinate chip. Three answer cards connect to it: zone and risk, distance to coast, address. |
| 0:46 | **Who it's for** | Insurance, property, logistics, anyone who needs to see where. |
| 0:54 | **Close** | "Tell us what you need to see on a map." Then the Webvoltz lockup and "Maps that make decisions clear." |

Captions run throughout and hide on the logo. Voice: synthetic male narrator; music: an ambient drone ducked under the voice; small clicks on each crosshair, a chime on the lookup pin. Mix normalised to −14 LUFS.
